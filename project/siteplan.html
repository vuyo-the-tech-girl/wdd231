E-Commerce Site Plan

Site Name

TrailGear Outfitters

Why this name: "TrailGear" clearly tells customers we sell outdoor/hiking equipment. "Outfitters" adds a trusted, expert feel. It’s short, memorable, and brands well with a mountain logo.

Optional domain: trailgear-outfitters.com

Site Purpose

The site provides an online store for outdoor enthusiasts to browse and purchase hiking, camping, and trail gear. It offers detailed product descriptions, customer reviews, sizing guides, and secure checkout. The site also includes a blog with trail guides and gear care tips to build community and drive traffic.

Target Audience Scenarios

These are questions real customers would ask. Answering them drives my content:

  • Scenario 1: "What size hiking backpack do I need for a 3-day trip, and do you have waterproof options?"
  • Scenario 2: "Where can I track my order after I check out, and what’s your return policy if the gear doesn’t fit?"

Typography

Using 2 Google Fonts for clean hierarchy:

  • Montserrat, sans-serif: All headings H1-H3. Bold, modern feel for branding.
  • Open Sans, sans-serif: Body text, paragraphs, lists. Highly readable for product descriptions.
