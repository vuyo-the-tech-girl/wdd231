E-Commerce Site Plan

Site Name

Blush & Tote

Why this name: "Blush" captures the pink color theme and soft, feminine vibe. "Tote" clearly tells customers we specialize in handbags, totes, and accessories. It's short, brandable, and pairs well with a minimalist handbag logo. Domain is easy to read.

Site Purpose

Blush & Tote is an online boutique for modern bags and accessories. We sell handbags, crossbody bags, totes, backpacks, and wallets for everyday use, work, and special events. The site provides detailed product photos, size/dimension guides, material info, customer reviews, and secure checkout.

Target Audience Scenarios

These are questions real customers would ask. Answering them drives our content:

  • Scenario 1: "I need a work tote that fits my 15-inch laptop + lunch. What are the exact dimensions and does it have an inside zip pocket?"
  • Scenario 2: "What’s your return policy if the color looks different in person? And how long does shipping take to East London?"

Typography

Using 2 Google Fonts for fashion + readability:

  • Playfair Display, serif: All headings H1-H3. Elegant, high-end feel for fashion branding and product names.
  • Lato, sans-serif: Body text, product descriptions, lists. Clean and highly readable on mobile.
